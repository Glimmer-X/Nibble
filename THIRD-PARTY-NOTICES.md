## Fusion Pixel Font

The pixel typeface of the interface: the 10 px and 12 px proportional Simplified Chinese builds of
[Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font) by TakWolf, release 2026.10.25,
in `Sources/NibbleUI/Resources/Fonts/`. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`
there).

Fusion Pixel combines these fonts, each under its own licence, kept beside the font files:

| Font | Licence file |

| Ark Pixel Font | `OFL-ark-pixel.txt` (SIL OFL 1.1) |
| Cubic 11 | `OFL-cubic-11.txt` (SIL OFL 1.1) |
| BoutiqueBitmap7x7 | `OFL-boutique-bitmap-7x7.txt` (SIL OFL 1.1) |
| BoutiqueBitmap9x9 | `OFL-boutique-bitmap-9x9.txt` (SIL OFL 1.1) |

The licence files are bundled into the app with the fonts.
